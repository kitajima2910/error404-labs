- Hien thi doan van can go, highlight tung tu
- Mau xanh cho tu dung, do cho tu sai
- Hien thi WPM va accuracy real-time

- Nhieu doan van mau (tieng Anh va tieng Viet)

- Luu ky luc cao nhat
