Hay tao mot ung dung tinh chi so BMI bang HTML, CSS va JavaScript thuan.

Yeu cau giao dien:
- Thiet ke y te, sach se, mau xanh duong/cyan
- Nhap chieu cao (cm hoac feet/inch) va can nang (kg hoac lbs)
- Hien thi BMI lon, ro rang voi mau sac theo muc do
- Thanh gauge/meter truc quan

Yeu cau chuc nang:
- Tinh BMI theo cong thuc chuan
- Phan loai: Thieu can / Binh thuong / Thua can / Beo phi
- Ho tro ca don vi metric va imperial
- Hien thi can nang ly tuong theo chieu cao
- Luu lich su do
