- Highlight cac ky tu duoc encode bang mau sac
- Hien thi so ky tu truoc/sau

- Phan tich URL thanh cac phan: protocol, host, path, query, hash
- Xay dung URL tu cac phan

- Hien thi bang ky tu dac biet va ma tuong ung
